About

CS is the path to learn full computer science in depth. Not a blog of highlights — a map of the field: what each idea is, why it matters, the mental model, and the mistakes that show up when you actually have to use it.

The catalog covers programming, systems, networks, databases, software engineering, security, distributed design, intelligence, and theory. Walk an ordered path or open the library.

There is no account and no server behind the pages. Theme preferences stay in your browser. The source is on GitHub.
