About

The Computer Science Handbook is a structured reference for learning computer science: what each idea is, why it matters, its mental model, and the mistakes that appear in practical use.

The catalog covers programming, systems, networks, databases, and related fields. Open the library to browse the available topics.

The handbook is free to read and does not require an account. Theme and reading preferences stay in your browser. The source is available on GitHub.
