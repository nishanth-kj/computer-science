About

CS is a free handbook for computer science. Each page covers one idea: what it is, why it matters, the mental model, and the mistakes that show up when you use it.

The catalog covers programming, systems, networks, databases, and related fields. Open the library to browse the available topics.

The handbook is free to read and does not require an account. Theme preferences stay in your browser. The source is available on GitHub.
