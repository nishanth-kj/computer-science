Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The Computer Science Handbook is an educational reference. This Privacy Policy describes browser storage and optional third-party services when they are configured and enabled.

1. Local Storage and Client-Side Data

You do not need to create an account or submit personal information to read the curriculum. Theme preferences and the current optional-services choice may be stored locally in your browser. Browser storage can be cleared using your browser settings.

2. Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising

Configured analytics and advertising scripts are not loaded until a visitor allows optional services through the site's current privacy banner. If enabled, those third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies and process technical information under their own policies. Declining keeps the site's configured optional analytics and advertising integrations disabled for that visit and future visits unless the saved choice is changed.

The site's built-in banner is a basic first-party choice control; it is not a Google-certified consent management platform and does not itself generate IAB Transparency and Consent Framework signals. Before serving personalised ads to people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the publisher must configure a Google-certified CMP that meets Google's current requirements. See Google's publisher CMP guidance.

3. Google AdSense and Advertising Cookies

AdSense is not enabled by default. If the publisher receives AdSense approval and configures a valid publisher ID and ad slot, the site can load Google advertising code after the visitor's optional-services choice. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, and measure ads. Review Google's advertising technologies policy. You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.

4. Affiliate Links and Sponsorships

Recommendations that earn commissions will be identified near the relevant link. Paid placements will be clearly labelled as sponsored and will not buy control over independent technical conclusions. See the Affiliate Disclosure page for details. Some links may lead to external sites whose privacy practices we do not control.

5. Hosting Logs and External Resources

Static hosting providers may process server access logs, including IP addresses, browser information, request times, and requested paths, to operate and secure the service. Fonts or other external assets may send requests to their respective providers under those providers' privacy terms.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You can decline optional services through the banner when it is shown, change browser storage settings, or clear the saved choice from your browser. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of personal information. This page is general information and is not legal advice; the publisher should review it against the services actually enabled and the laws that apply.

7. External Links

Educational pages may link to external documentation, repositories, publishers, course platforms, and other sites. We do not control their content or privacy practices.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email neurokodes@gmail.com, visit the Contact page, or open the project issue tracker.
