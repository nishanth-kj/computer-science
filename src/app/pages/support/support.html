Educational sponsorships

We consider partnerships with technical publishers, developer-tool teams, and computer-science education providers. Sponsorship opportunities may include a clearly labelled sponsored tutorial, a subject-specific learning resource, or an educational tool demonstration that is genuinely useful to readers. Each proposal is reviewed for relevance and fit; not every category will be available at every time.

  • Paid placements will be labelled clearly as sponsored.
  • Sponsors cannot buy favourable technical conclusions, rankings, or control of independent explanations.
  • Sponsored links and claims will be checked for relevance and presented without misleading guarantees.
  • Packages, dates, and pricing are discussed case by case. We do not publish audience or impression claims until they can be verified from current analytics.

To discuss a partnership, email send a sponsorship inquiry. Include your organization, campaign goal, audience, timeline, campaign URL, and (optionally) budget range.
