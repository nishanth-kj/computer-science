Affiliate Disclosure

Last reviewed: October 2026

The Computer Science Handbook does not currently intentionally publish affiliate links. This page is published in advance so any future commercial recommendations have a clear disclosure location.

If affiliate links are introduced

Links that may earn a commission will be clearly identified near the recommendation before a reader clicks. A commission will not increase the reader's price unless the page explicitly says so. Recommendations should be relevant to the topic and based on educational usefulness, not only on commission rate.

Sponsored content

Paid sponsorships will be labelled separately from editorial explanations. A sponsor will not be allowed to purchase a favourable technical conclusion or control the handbook's educational content.
