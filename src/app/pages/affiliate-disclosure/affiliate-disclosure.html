Affiliate and Sponsorship Disclosure

Last reviewed: October 2026

The Computer Science Handbook aims to keep its core learning material free and useful. Commercial links and paid placements are not a current guarantee that a page contains an affiliate relationship or sponsor; disclosures will appear when a specific relationship exists.

Affiliate links

If a link earns a commission, it will be identified close to the recommendation before the reader clicks. Recommendations should be relevant to the topic and based on educational usefulness rather than commission rate. The price to the reader may vary only where the page explicitly states that fact.

Sponsored content

Paid articles, tutorials, tools, or placements will be labelled clearly as sponsored. A sponsor cannot buy a favourable technical conclusion, change independent rankings, or control an educational explanation. Sponsored content will be reviewed for relevance and obvious conflicts of interest.

Advertising

Display advertising may be shown only if an advertising account is approved and the site owner has completed the required configuration and consent setup. Advertisements are separate from editorial explanations.
